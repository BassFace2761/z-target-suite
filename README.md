# Z-Targeting Suite for Majora's Mask: Recompiled

A collection of highly customizable tweaks to modernize, enhance, and streamline the Z-Targeting mechanics in *The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask*.

## Features
* **Immersive Targeting:** Removes non-diegetic visual cues (targeting reticles, yellow hover arrows) and audio cues (lock-on and lock-off sound effects). Rely solely on Tatl's position and color for a purely cinematic combat experience!
* **360-Degree Targeting:** Lock onto enemies and objects behind you, bypassing the vanilla camera's field-of-view restrictions. Off-screen targeting range is carefully balanced to 65% of the standard range to prevent accidental cross-map camera snaps.
* **Auto-Retargeting:** When your current target is defeated, the camera will automatically snap to the next nearest enemy.
* **Instant Target Break:** Removes the short delay when breaking a lock-on, making camera control feel much snappier when releasing Z or when an enemy is defeated.
* **Faster Aim Tracking:** Link now rotates to aim at targets much faster, completely bypassing the artificial delay caused by the vanilla UI animations.
